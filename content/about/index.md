# About Me

I'm Tom and this is my static blog. I'm currently working on The Receivables Company. Having seen and talked with a lot of people stuck doing manual work to collect cash that should have been paid, I figured how hard could it be to automate it.

I enjoy travelling to Japan every year and taking photos (the photo above was taken at Hokkaido University), and having been putting on and off learning the language. I also like to make espressos with beans roasted from around the world, with a preference for local roasteries.
